Welcome, fellow seeker of knowledge, to my personal collection of arcane writings. Across countless generations, sorcerers have devoted their lives to understanding the mysteries of magic. Their discoveries, experiments, and teachings have been preserved within the pages of these ancient volumes. Some manuscripts contain the foundations of elemental sorcery, while others document rituals whose origins have long since disappeared from history. Each book represents the knowledge of those who came before us, and each page offers another glimpse into the boundless nature of the arcane arts.

"The beginning of wisdom is the definition of terms." — Socrates

The first volume introduces the fundamental principles upon which the arcane arts are built. It explores the origins of magical energy, the relationship between the natural world and sorcery, and the methods through which practitioners learn to manipulate unseen forces. Early scholars believed that magic was not an arbitrary power, but rather a discipline governed by rules and principles waiting to be understood.

Through careful study and experimentation, the earliest sorcerers established the foundations of spellcraft. Their discoveries would eventually shape the practices of generations to come.

"The limits of my language mean the limits of my world." — Ludwig Wittgenstein

The second volume examines the written language of magic. Throughout history, sorcerers have developed elaborate systems of symbols, inscriptions, and incantations to record and perform their craft. These writings are more than simple instructions. Every symbol carries meaning, and every incantation represents a carefully constructed arrangement of words and intention.

However, translating these ancient scriptures has proven to be a difficult undertaking. Languages have changed, civilizations have vanished, and countless spells have been lost to the passage of time. What remains are fragments of a much greater body of knowledge.

"There are more things in heaven and earth, Horatio, than are dreamt of in your philosophy." — William Shakespeare

The final volume contains writings that were deliberately excluded from ordinary magical education. Within its pages are records of forbidden rituals, unstable enchantments, and experiments that challenged the boundaries of conventional sorcery. Unlike the previous volumes, these manuscripts offer little explanation of their contents. Many passages are incomplete, their meanings obscured by coded language and unfamiliar symbols.

Whether these writings represent the greatest achievements of ancient sorcerers or the consequences of their greatest mistakes remains uncertain. For now, they shall remain preserved within this collection, awaiting a reader capable of understanding their contents.

A Message from the Keeper

Knowledge is perhaps the greatest instrument ever possessed by a sorcerer. Yet its pursuit demands patience, discipline, and an understanding of the responsibilities that accompany discovery. These writings represent centuries of study and experimentation, preserved so that future generations may continue the work of those who came before them.

I encourage every reader to approach these volumes with an open mind and a questioning spirit. For magic, much like knowledge itself, reveals its secrets only to those willing to seek them.

— Sorc. Ersen, Keeper of the Arcanum
