VOLUME MMXXVI THE AFTERLIFE COLLECTION

A MESSAGE FROM THE CURATOR

Where Silence Becomes Poetry

Welcome, wanderer, to a place where the boundary between life and death has long since faded. Here, the departed are not forgotten. Their thoughts linger in verses, their memories survive in ink, and their final words remain etched upon the walls of eternity.

This collection gathers elegies, reflections, and verses inspired by the afterlife. Each manuscript is a fragment of something once living, preserved long after its final breath. Read carefully. Even the dead have something left to say.

— Sorc. Matthew

VOLUME I

The Last Lament

On the passing of life

MANUSCRIPT I ✦
THE LAST LAMENT 01 / 03

Every life ends in silence, but not every silence is empty. There are names that linger in the air, footsteps that remain upon forgotten roads, and memories that refuse to surrender to time.

The first manuscript explores the final moments of existence and the emotions left behind. It considers grief not as an ending, but as a quiet testament to the lives that once mattered.

Perhaps the greatest tragedy of death is not that a voice disappears, but that the world continues without hearing it.

"The dead are never truly silent; they speak through those who remember."

— A fragment from the forgotten
VOLUME II

Beyond the Veil

Whispers from the afterlife

MANUSCRIPT II ✦
BEYOND THE VEIL 02 / 03

What lies beyond the final breath? Is there a kingdom of endless light, an ocean of darkness, or merely an eternal dream from which no one awakens?

The second manuscript wanders through the uncertain territories of the afterlife. Its verses imagine what may exist beyond the veil, where earthly worries dissolve and the boundaries of reality become indistinct.

Here, death is no longer simply an ending. It becomes a passage into the unknown, where every answer gives rise to another question.

"Perhaps eternity is not a place, but a moment that never ends."

— A whisper beyond the veil
VOLUME III

What Remains

Memory beyond eternity

MANUSCRIPT III ✦
WHAT REMAINS 03 / 03

Long after the body has returned to dust, something remains. A story told by a stranger, a letter preserved in a drawer, a familiar melody carried through generations.

The final manuscript reflects upon remembrance and the strange immortality granted by memory. Though the living eventually depart, the traces they leave behind continue to shape the world.

Perhaps this is the closest humanity comes to defeating death: not by living forever, but by leaving something worth remembering.

"A life may end, but its echoes need not follow it into silence."

— The final remembrance

THE CURATOR'S FINAL WORDS

Until the Last Echo Fades

You have reached the end of these manuscripts, but perhaps not the end of their meaning. The voices preserved here may be distant, their authors long departed, yet their words remain capable of stirring the living.

Remember that every epitaph tells two stories: one of the person who has passed, and another of those who remain.

May their words never rest. — Sorc. Matthew
