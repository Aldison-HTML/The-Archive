Welcome, traveler, to my private archive. Within these walls lie fragments of forgotten knowledge, preserved through ink and parchment. Every scroll holds a discovery, every manuscript a story, and every inscription a secret waiting to be uncovered.

"The greatest enemy of knowledge is not ignorance, but the illusion of knowledge." — Stephen Hawking

My first manuscript, carefully transcribed and preserved within these archives. It marks the beginning of my studies, a record of the principles and discoveries that shaped my understanding of the world. Though its pages may appear ordinary, they hold the foundation upon which greater knowledge is built.

Do not mistake an unfinished work for a worthless one. Even the most accomplished sorcerers began with a single line of ink and an unanswered question.

"It is not in the stars to hold our destiny but in ourselves." — William Shakespeare

The second scroll carries the continuation of my research. Here, earlier observations are expanded, familiar principles are challenged, and new connections begin to emerge. Knowledge is rarely discovered in a single moment; it is accumulated through curiosity, patience, and experimentation.

There remains much to uncover. These archives shall continue to grow, as each new discovery brings another piece of the greater mystery into light.

"The only true wisdom is in knowing you know nothing." — Socrates

A Final Word from the Curator

Knowledge is not something one simply possesses. It is something pursued, questioned, and refined. These pages represent only a fraction of what I have yet to discover. Should you find something of value within them, then this archive has served its purpose.

— Sorc. Aldison
